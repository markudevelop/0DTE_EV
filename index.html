Notes: C is credit in index points (e.g., 0.80). t is % of credit (50 = take half). m is stop × credit (2.0 = 2× credit). Formulas: +EV threshold p* = m/(m+t). Kelly f* = p − (m/t)(1−p). EV (credits) = p·t − (1−p)·m. Dollar EV = EV·C·multiplier. Risk/Spread @ stop = min(m, (W−C)/C)·C·multiplier. Max expiry loss = (W − C)·multiplier.
