0DTE Credit-Spread Kelly & +EV Calculator

What this tool is

A sizing and optimization helper for 0DTE credit spreads and iron condors. It combines edge (+EV), Kelly sizing, broker Buying Power, and a Max Daily Loss guardrail so you can cap downside while recycling capital intraday.
You can use it to:
  • Compute +EV threshold, Kelly fraction, and $EV per unit
  • Constrain size by BP (Reg‑T or PM) and Max Daily Loss
  • Optimize profit target (t) and stop (m) across grids
  • Model iron condor overlap risk via ρ and recycle wins per day
Quick steps:
  1. Set credit, width, and your win rate
  2. Choose target t (% credit) and stop m (× credit)
  3. Enter Bankroll, BP, Net Liq, and Max Daily Loss %
  4. Select Reg‑T or plug your PM $/unit
  5. Use the Simulator to scan t/m and pick the max EV/day
Notes: C is credit in index points (e.g., 0.80). t is % of credit (50 = take half). m is stop × credit (2.0 = 2× credit). If W and C imply a max loss smaller than your stop, m is capped at (W − C)/C. “Custom PM” lets you plug your broker’s live portfolio‑margin haircut per unit; Reg‑T uses max loss. Max Daily Loss caps unit count so worst‑case realized losses do not exceed the % of Net Liq for the day.
Formulas: +EV threshold p* = m/(m+t). Kelly f* = p − (m/t)(1−p). EV (credits) = p·t − (1−p)·m. Dollar EV = EV·C·multiplier. Round‑trip fees = legs × (feeOpen + feeClose) [legs: spread=2, IC=4]. Expected slippage/trade = C·mult·[sO + sC·(p·(1−t) + (1−p)·m)], where sO=slipOpen%, sC=slipClose%. Net EV$ = Dollar EV − fees − expected slippage. Risk/Spread @ stop = min(m, (W−C)/C)·C·multiplier. Max expiry loss = (W − C)·multiplier. Max Daily Loss cap N ≤ (MDL$ / worst_losses_per_unit).
